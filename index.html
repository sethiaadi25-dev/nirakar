    From
    nothing

    Nirakar is a small, multidisciplinary studio. We design games, build 3D worlds and sets, animate, make motion graphics, shape brands and design clothes.

    The name is Sanskrit for formless. That’s where every project starts: an idea with no shape yet. Our job is to give it one, whether that’s a playable city, a frame, a mark or a garment.

    We show our process as well as the finished work. Most of what you see here is still in progress.
